The GREEN Baseline Pipeline

How HiperPulse extracts optical signals, tests verification gates, and honestly withholds uncertain estimates.

STAGE 01

Dual Cheek Patches

Fixed guide boxes extract spatial green-channel mean intensities from left and right cheek skin, minimizing specular reflection and micro-motion.

STAGE 02

Signal Detrending & Filter

A 4th-order Butterworth bandpass filter (0.75–3.0 Hz / 45–180 BPM) attenuates low-frequency baseline drifts and high-frequency sensor noise.

STAGE 03

FFT & Autocorrelation

Dual independent peak estimators (spectral density peak + temporal lag autocorrelation) must agree within 8.0 BPM to ensure fundamental periodicity.

STAGE 04

14 Heuristic Quality Gates

Every candidate signal is scrutinized across lighting, timing, bilateral agreement, SNR, and stability. Any single failure forces an honest null BPM.
