Scientific Principles & Boundary Disclaimers

1. Mathematical GREEN Baseline

HiperPulse extracts raw spatial mean RGB pixel traces from two bilateral cheek regions of interest. By isolating the Green spectral band (~530 nm) where oxygenated hemoglobin absorption produces peak optical photoplethysmographic modulation, subtle skin reflectance fluctuations are isolated over time.

2. Strict Multi-Gate Verification

Estimates are gated by 14 deterministic heuristic checks: signal continuity, PTS timing jitter (< 20 ms), exposure range (20–235), clipping fraction (< 5%), bilateral inter-patch discordance (≤ 8 BPM), temporal half-split stability, and dominant spectral power concentration.

3. Honest Ethical Limits

Heuristic check passage does not prove biological arterial origin. Synthetic test fixtures and shared optical lighting easily pass heuristic gates. HiperPulse is an experimental wellness demonstration, not a medical diagnostic device.
